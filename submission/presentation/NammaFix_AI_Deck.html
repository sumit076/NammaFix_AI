01 · Problem

Bengaluru's Civic Complaint Crisis

13 million residents. 10,000–20,000 monthly complaints. A system that isn't keeping up.

Duplication Overload

30–45% of complaints are duplicates of the same issue. Multiple field teams dispatched to the same pothole — wasting public money.

No Intelligent Triage

All complaints queue equally. A cracked pavement near a school at night waits beside a minor pothole on a quiet lane.

Wrong Department Routing

BBMP road complaints sent to BESCOM. Drainage issues sent to SWM. Mis-routing costs 3–5 extra days per complaint before the right team is engaged.

Zero Citizen Feedback

After submitting, citizens receive no status updates, no resolution timeline and no visibility into which department is responsible.

03 · AI Pipeline

5-Stage Agent Pipeline

Transparent, deterministic, explainable — every decision traced back to its rule.

1
Classification Agent
20+ weighted regex rules across 4 categories. Image filename scanned for hints (no vision model). Flags citizen/AI disagreement for officer review. Outputs category + confidence score.
2
Duplicate Detection Agent
Haversine geometry: same category + within 250 m + last 30 days + unresolved. All matched reports merged into oldest incident. Every original Complaint ID preserved. Prevents N field-team dispatches for 1 problem.
3
Severity Assessment Agent
35% Safety · 25% Importance · 25% Population · 15% Duration. 10 safety signal types. Live-recomputed as reports accumulate and time passes. Critical ≥75 → 24h SLA.
4
Department Routing Agent
Category → BBMP department (Roads / SWM / BESCOM / Storm Drain). Locality → BBMP administrative zone. Zero mis-routing.
5
Action Recommendation Agent
Generates urgency headline, SLA in hours, 3-step action checklist per category. Advisory only — requires officer approval before dispatch.

03 · AI Pipeline — Severity Detail

Severity Scoring: Transparent by Design

Every score is deterministic, reproducible and auditable. No black box.

Priority Score = 0.35 × Safety Risk (0–100)
                 + 0.25 × Public Importance (0–100)
                 + 0.25 × Affected Population (0–100)
                 + 0.15 × Unresolved Duration (0–100)
Critical
Score ≥ 75

SLA: 24 hours

High
Score 50–74

SLA: 72 hours

Medium
Score 25–49

SLA: 1 week

Low
Score < 25

SLA: 2 weeks

Safety signals detected (10 categories): accident/injury, falls/skidding, two-wheelers, deep/wide hazard, electrical/manhole risk, stalling vehicles, night-time risk, children/elderly nearby, traffic disruption, public health (dengue, stray animals, sewage).

Priority is live-recomputed every minute — a "Medium" issue ignored for 3 weeks automatically escalates to "High".
